# Sound remote Skycache validation without repeated dependency expansion

Research cutoff: 26 October 2026. Research and planning only; no source changes or builds. Evidence below distinguishes peer-reviewed research, preprints, official documentation, informal engineering notes, and proposals derived for this project.

## What does recent research establish, and what transfers to Skycache?

### Takeaway

The promising transfer is to reduce how much state must be observed and how often it is validated, while preserving an explicit proof of dependency completeness. Recent research supports compact exact indexing, effect-aware reuse, and difference-sized synchronization; it does not make workspace freshness free or establish a speedup for this branch.

### Cited Findings

1. **Bazel Skyframe documentation — official living documentation, checked 26 October 2026.** Hermeticity depends on recording every input dependency. Skyframe distinguishes bottom-up invalidation from top-down checking and uses change pruning when recomputation produces an unchanged value. This is the authoritative correctness boundary for a remote validator. [Bazel Skyframe](https://bazel.build/reference/skyframe)

2. **Build Systems à la Carte — ICFP, September 2018, peer-reviewed foundational research; expanded journal version 2020.** Separates scheduling from rebuilding and gives an executable framework for comparing build systems. Useful conceptual distinction: fetching/deserializing a candidate and proving that it can be reused are independent decisions. [Microsoft Research](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/build-systems-la-carte/)

3. **Incr: Faster Re-Execution via Bolt-On Incrementalization — OSDI, July 2026, peer reviewed.** Reuses shell computation through dependency/effect tracking. Its paper explicitly separates exactly memoizable effects, detectable effects requiring conservative reruns, blocked effects, ignored effects, and out-of-scope behavior. Supported effects include regular files, directories, symlinks, hard links and metadata; some environmental/device/network effects require rerunning. Transfer the typed observation taxonomy and conservative fallback, not its shell benchmark speedups or entire tracing architecture. [USENIX paper](https://www.usenix.org/system/files/osdi26-xie-yizheng.pdf), [venue/date](https://www.usenix.org/conference/osdi26/presentation/xie-yizheng)

4. **Automated Dependency Optimization for Artifact-Based Build Systems / DepReduce — PACMSE ISSTA, October 2026, peer reviewed; conference presentation 8 October.** Formalizes cumulative rebuild cost and proves dependency lifting/flattening correct and optimal under its stated objective. Latest author PDF evaluates 19 projects, whereas the conference abstract still says 14; use the final paper and avoid mixing numbers. Transfer its insistence on a precise objective and preservation proof. It optimizes declared artifact dependencies; it does not authorize dropping dynamic Skyframe witnesses or prove a remote certificate transformation sound. [Final paper](https://xuhongxu.com/issta26-depreduce.pdf), [conference page](https://conf.researchr.org/details/issta-2026/issta-2026-research-papers/100/Automated-Dependency-Optimization-for-Artifact-Based-Build-Systems)

5. **A Comparative Study of Compressed, Learned, and Traditional Indexing Methods for Integer Data — SEA, 15 July 2025, peer reviewed.** Benchmarks static integer indexes across 12 datasets. SIMD B-tree variants outperform the studied alternatives in lookup speed; highly compressed LA-vector/Elias–Fano representations trade lookup speed for space. Construction and scan costs are separately measured. This is evidence to benchmark representation choices on real witness-ID distributions, not assume maximum compression gives minimum build time. It is not a Java or Skyframe benchmark. [Paper and publication record](https://drops.dagstuhl.de/entities/document/10.4230/LIPIcs.SEA.2025.5)

6. **Ribbon: Fast Succinct Static Retrieval and Approximate Membership — Journal of the ACM 73(1), published 13 February 2026, peer reviewed.** PDF front matter confirms the date; some secondary aggregators show inconsistent dates. Retrieval returns the associated value for members but arbitrary values outside the stored set. Approximate membership permits false positives; near-succinct space comes with construction/query tradeoffs. A retrieval structure cannot stand alone as a validity memo. [Publisher PDF at KIT](https://publikationen.bibliothek.kit.edu/1000191446/177619178), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1145/3785417)

7. **ZOR Filters: Fast and Smaller Than Fuse Filters — SEA, 15 June 2026, peer reviewed; initial preprint 3 February.** Deterministic continuation of peeling guarantees construction termination, preserving false-positive-only membership semantics through an auxiliary structure. The initial prototype has a construction-cost disadvantage. Deterministic construction is not exact membership. Consider only after an actual memory bottleneck warrants a filter. [Publication record](https://drops.dagstuhl.de/entities/document/10.4230/LIPIcs.SEA.2026.24), [initial preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.03525)

8. **Practical Rateless Set Reconciliation — SIGCOMM, 4–8 August 2024, peer reviewed; revised preprint 19 June.** Rateless IBLTs communicate a stream sized to the set difference without requiring its size in advance. Density-evolution analysis gives an asymptotic 1.35 coded symbols per difference under the paper's model. Decoding purity uses a checksum with nonzero collision probability. This is a candidate transport optimization for already-materialized immutable manifests, not an independent certificate of current filesystem equality. [Full paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2402.02668v3)

9. **CertainSync: Rateless Set Reconciliation with Certainty — arXiv preprint, 11 April 2025; no peer-reviewed publication verified.** Uses coding/group-testing constructions with guaranteed listing in a finite universe under explicit conditions. Its UniverseReduceSync extension reintroduces hash collisions, estimation and verification tradeoffs. Original-universe guarantees can be costly for a 256-bit digest universe. Do not describe the entire family as collision-free. [Version/date](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.08314), [full text, especially sections 3 and 6](https://arxiv.org/html/2504.08314v1)

10. **Skycache unconference notes — meeting 14 October 2026, gist created 19 October; informal primary engineering notes, not a paper or reproducible benchmark.** Describes storing dependency DAGs, comparing VCS changes with paths/directories, memoizing invalidation work, and pruning client graph expansion. This is a close architectural precedent for a dependency certificate plus change-oracle service. Reported production improvements are not transferable to our branch; the notes omit enough detail that they cannot establish our required completeness invariant. [Meeting notes](https://gist.github.com/oquenchil/1da2b1993a68501d37a2bade6f4be8e5)

### Inferences

- **Optimize existing exact reuse before advanced filters.** The current validator already memoizes successful roots, locks per root, defers shared in-flight dependencies during iterative DFS, and reuses witnesses within an invocation. A primitive-ID/state representation is therefore an incremental experiment in key storage, capacity and remaining duplicate scope, not a proposal to add command-scoped memoization. Proceed only if profiling shows residual repeated work or representation cost. [Current validator](https://github.com/JSGette/bazel/blob/bf6b717195/src/main/java/com/google/devtools/build/lib/skyframe/serialization/local/LocalDependencyValidator.java#L124-L250)
- **Treat observations by semantics.** Use distinct typed witnesses for content, existence/nonexistence, directory membership, symlink resolution, configuration/toolchain identity, external-repository identity and environment. Incr's effect classes motivate this discipline; the exact Skycache supported/unsupported boundary must be derived from the implementation.
- **Optimize proof cost separately from graph size.** DepReduce's rebuild-cost objective is useful inspiration, but minimizing serialized edge count can increase decode work, union construction, random accesses or cold misses. A Skycache objective must charge all of these.
- **Do not import headline speedups.** None of these papers demonstrates performance for this Java implementation, linked-worktree scope, local chunk cache, or target workload.

### Gaps

- No externally verified paper supplies a ready-made sound remote Skyframe certificate implementation covering this branch's full environment model.
- No reliable evidence found that a learned index, GPU set intersection, recursive cryptographic proof, or dynamic transitive-closure algorithm would outperform a simple exact primitive representation at the observed scale. Exclude these from the initial plan.
- Informal upstream notes are useful design clues but lack a formal change-feed completeness contract and a comparable benchmark.

## What mathematical invariants permit a hit without expanding its entire graph?

### Takeaway

A stored certificate can eliminate repeated traversal only if it faithfully represents every observation that could affect the cached result. Sound acceptance still needs either fresh observation of its unique witnesses or a complete change oracle relating the baseline to the current workspace.

### Cited Findings

- Skyframe correctness requires registered data dependencies, and its documented invalidation strategies depend on knowing changed inputs or checking a reachable closure. [Official semantics](https://bazel.build/reference/skyframe)
- The current project documentation retains per-entry validation, global external/absolute-path evidence, identity isolation, and publication revalidation; its next-step guidance explicitly preserves those guards. [Current project document](<//wsl.localhost/Ubuntu/home/jgett/REPOS/bazel-skycache-remote-mvp/docs/jsgette/skycache-remote-mvp.md>)
- Approximate membership guarantees have a direction: a correctly built filter can exclude nonmembers safely, but positive answers may be false. Static retrieval is weaker still for queries outside its key set. [Ribbon](https://publikationen.bibliothek.kit.edu/1000191446/177619178)

### Inferences

**Derived model, not a theorem claimed by a cited paper.** Let `W_e` be the complete set of typed input observations sufficient for cached entry `e`; each includes its expected baseline value. Let `I_e` bind serializer/schema, repository namespace, configuration, toolchain, external-repository and environment semantics. Let `E` identify the command's validated filesystem/environment epoch. A sufficient acceptance condition is:

`compatible(I_e, current) AND complete(W_e) AND for every w in W_e: observed(w,E) = expected(w,e)`.

Completeness includes observations controlling dependency discovery. A file that did not exist, a directory membership test, and a symlink target can change which dependency would have been requested. Treating only previously read regular-file content as `W_e` is insufficient.

If a trusted process provides a complete set `D` of changed typed observations between the certificate baseline and `E`, acceptance can instead use `W_e ∩ D = empty`, with identity and completeness checks. A version-control diff alone is not this oracle: untracked files, generated inputs, ignored files, missing-path creation, external repositories, environment/configuration changes and symlink resolution need coverage or explicit conservative rejection.

**Cold-start lower bound, elementary indistinguishability argument.** With arbitrary independent mutable witnesses and no trusted maintained summary/change feed, an algorithm that never observes one witness cannot distinguish the unchanged workspace from one in which only that witness changed. Exact worst-case validation therefore needs one observation per relevant unique witness. Hashing a stored certificate does not evade this: it proves stored-byte identity, not freshness. Incremental indexes move work to update/priming time and amortize it; they do not erase it.

**Work reduction model, not a description of the current baseline.** Let `R` count all repeated witness references, `U` the unique compatible witnesses requested this command, `A` certificate edges actually traversed, and `d=|D|`. A hypothetical traversal without reuse costs roughly `c_edge A + c_observe R`; exact command memoization aims at `c_edge A + c_observe U`. Current Skycache already has invocation-local reuse, so the relevant remaining quantities are duplicate scope across stages, memo saturation, lookup/key allocation and graph/record processing after memo hits. A maintained change index may reduce observation work toward `d`, but only after charging change discovery, index maintenance, epoch checks and intersection queries. Measure these coefficients instead of assuming they are equal.

**Safe filter orientation.** A no-false-negative filter representing complete `W_e` may be probed for every element of complete `D`. If every probe is negative, the sets are disjoint. Any positive means 'possibly affected' and triggers exact checking or a miss. This never accepts a stale hit because of an ordinary false positive. Conversely, a Bloom/Ribbon/ZOR positive cannot mean 'already validated clean'. Corruption, incomplete construction, incompatible ID dictionaries and missing changes still require rejection. For a truly disjoint pair, a rough independence model gives probability `1-(1-p)^d` of at least one false positive; for large `d`, even small `p` can erase usefulness. This formula is a sizing heuristic, not a correctness assumption.

**Certificate design constraint.** Eagerly storing every entry's full transitive leaf set can expand toward quadratic total metadata on heavily shared DAGs. Prefer shared canonical witness blocks and exact certificate DAGs, lazily traversed and memoized. A proposed cut/frontier is valid only if its certificates cover every relevant root-to-observation path. Shared DAG costs make naive tree dynamic programming and greedy edge deletion unsafe as optimization proofs.

**Lifetime constraint.** A valid result is bound to its compatible certificate/dictionary and command epoch, not just its node ID. Reusing a dense integer ID after switching publication, changing witness expectation or invalidating the epoch must not inherit 'valid'. Concurrent validation must distinguish unknown/in-progress/valid/invalid and preserve existing cycle/error/cancellation semantics. Any snapshot or watcher overflow means fallback/revalidation, not 'no changes'.

### Gaps

- The exact completeness of the available Git/workspace change feed has not been established, so a first remote reader must retain full witness validation as fallback.
- Whole-build consequences of avoiding graph materialization depend on how remote values participate in later Skyframe invalidation, rewinding and analysis/execution. A compact certificate alone does not prove those lifecycle semantics.
- Current byte hashes should remain the existing integrity primitive. Introducing probabilistic reconciliation must not quietly replace it with a weaker checksum-based acceptance rule.

## Which experiments should be planned, and what should stop them?

### Takeaway

Stage attribution comes first, representation and remaining-redundancy improvements to existing exact validation second, and graph-eliding certificate experiments third. Treat filters and reconciliation as conditional follow-ups because retained-warm runs already avoid blob downloads and publication remains in the timed path.

### Cited Findings

- The internal audit reports the measured product revision as `b368beb0ddde` and later clean HEAD as `bf6b717195`. Latest six retained-warm remote samples downloaded zero blob bytes, reused 1,604 chunks / roughly 1.462 GB locally, and still uploaded roughly 10.26 MB. Raw rows contain roughly 790,700 shared-CAS gets, 603,428 validator records, 182,455 checked witnesses, 305,730 witness memo hits and 249,870 admission-witness memo hits; publication is in the timed command. The memo hits demonstrate that reuse already exists. These workload-specific counts do not prove validation causes the entire warm gap. [Raw measurements](https://github.com/JSGette/bazel/blob/bf6b717195/docs/jsgette/measurements/skycache-retained-chunks-agent-2026-10-25/comparison.json)
- Successful-root memoization, per-root locks, iterative DFS with shared in-flight dependency deferral, and invocation-local witness reuse are already implemented. Negative outcomes do not persist, and publication independently revalidates inputs. Preserve these semantics; do not count implementing them again as an optimization. [Current validator](https://github.com/JSGette/bazel/blob/bf6b717195/src/main/java/com/google/devtools/build/lib/skyframe/serialization/local/LocalDependencyValidator.java#L124-L250)
- Prior compact-certificate results supplied by the coordinator show substantial validator/allocation improvement without a consistent full-build gain. Therefore a validator microbenchmark is insufficient as the acceptance criterion. The audit owner should supply the exact earlier artifact link in the final report.
- The 2025 indexing study measures construction cost independently of access cost, and the 2026 filter work likewise exposes construction/query/space tradeoffs. Those costs belong in the experiment budget. [SEA 2025 study](https://drops.dagstuhl.de/entities/document/10.4230/LIPIcs.SEA.2025.5), [ZOR](https://drops.dagstuhl.de/entities/document/10.4230/LIPIcs.SEA.2026.24)

### Inferences

**P0 — attribute the complete critical path.** Plan trace spans/counters for candidate discovery; metadata decode; local/remote value reads; authentication and record validation; typed witness observations; unique versus repeated validation; positive-memo occupancy/evictions; deserialization; graph insertion; and publication. Capture CPU, allocation, filesystem bytes/syscalls, wait time and wall time with overlap-aware accounting. Test cold remote, retained chunks, warm process, and controlled publication modes. Do not add overlapping spans to claim a total. Acceptance: reproducible stage attribution that explains enough of the warm gap to choose one intervention; no implementation authorized by this planning task.

**P1 — incremental representation and residual-redundancy experiment.** Keep the existing successful-root memoization, per-root coordination, iterative DFS deferral, invocation-local witness reuse and publication revalidation. After measuring remaining costs, compare publication-scoped canonical IDs and compact exact positive/state representations against current key/hash/object structures using recorded traces. Preserve complete-key equality during interning, expected-value identity, current lock/lifetime semantics and the lack of persistent negative results; a short hash is only an accelerator. Measure positive-cap saturation/eviction before changing capacity. Investigate overlap between admission and entry validation only where they share exactly the same observation semantics and stable epoch; publication's later freshness check must remain independent. State bits alone for 603,428 records are about 147 KiB at two bits per record, but dictionary, concurrency and certificate storage are additional and must be measured. Acceptance: demonstrate which existing repeated work, key allocation or lookup cost disappears; retain all correctness-required rechecks; achieve lower end-to-end warm median without material p95/memory regression. Stop if current reuse already eliminates the relevant duplication or synchronization/dictionary cost consumes the gain. This is not a proposal for new command-scoped memoization.

**P2 — compact shared certificates and optional complete-delta intersection.** Compare a compact/lazy proof representation against the existing iterative, shared DAG traversal without changing acceptance semantics. Shared traversal itself is already present; the proposed benefit must come from fewer authenticated record reads, smaller decode/key overhead, or a demonstrated reduction in graph materialization. Then, only where a complete change source exists, compare exact sorted-ID or bitmap intersection against the current validator. Select sorted primitive arrays for tiny sparse sets, bitmap/container candidates for larger clustered sets, and measure crossover rather than mandate a format. Charge certificate creation, publication bytes, decode, root lookup and retained memory. Reject eager all-entry transitive-set materialization if metadata expands excessively. Acceptance: avoids actual graph expansion/reads, not just re-encoding the same work; output/action graph agrees with uncached baseline; end-to-end gains reproduce on representative large builds. Stop when proof-data production/storage exceeds saved read work.

**P3 — bounded, cost-aware certificate traversal.** If certificates remain expensive, evaluate rejection-likelihood/cost ordering only where it adds to the existing independent-work-first handling of shared in-flight dependencies. Cheap common incompatibilities and highly discriminating witnesses may fail a candidate earlier. Charge ordering metadata and sorting/scheduling cost. This is a deterministic ordering experiment with unchanged logical conjunction, not a proposal to add existing dependency deferral. Avoid a new general dynamic reachability engine unless traces show repeated graph work dominates exact leaf observations. Acceptance: same verdicts for every candidate and reduced cost on both positive and negative mixes; no cold-positive regression.

**Deferred — Ribbon/ZOR filters and Rateless/CertainSync.** Filters become attractive only if exact dependency metadata exceeds a measured memory budget or expensive irrelevant blocks dominate. They must use the safe direction above and fall back on positives. Set reconciliation becomes attractive only if large overlapping immutable manifests must be exchanged over a costly network and maintained encodings already exist; rebuilding both sketches from all entries can cancel the gain. Validate any reconciled immutable state against the existing trusted digest/root and use bounded fallback. CertainSync's exact finite-universe theory is useful research context, but the preliminary large-digest implementation is not the next production step.

**Correctness matrix for every proposed validator change.** Plan paired cached/uncached tests for content edits with preserved size/timestamp; file deletion and rename; creation of a formerly absent file; directory/glob changes; symlink retargeting and chains; configuration transitions and toolchains; external-repository content and bindings; environment changes; cancellation and concurrent readers; epoch rollover; watcher overflow; missing/corrupt certificate blocks; and ID reuse across manifests. Expected outcomes are exact hits for truly unaffected entries, conservative misses otherwise, and identical verified outputs. These are future acceptance requirements, not tests run here.

**Promotion gate.** Predeclare representative workloads and smallest useful effect after P0; use randomized paired run order, enough repetitions for uncertainty intervals, and the same filesystem/process/cache reset semantics across arms. Track publication separately and in user-visible command latency. Amdahl's law bounds any claim: if measured validator share is `f` and its speedup is `s`, ideal total speedup is at most `1/((1-f)+f/s)` before new overhead. A strong validator win with weak build gain should remain an experiment, as the earlier certificate result already illustrates.

### Gaps

- Current causal stage timing and unique-reference overlap are the deciding missing data. The observed warm penalty cannot responsibly be assigned entirely to validation, deserialization, disk access or publication yet.
- The earlier 418k witness figure differs from the latest audit's 182,455 checked witnesses; likely scopes/publications differ. Do not mix them as a trend without matching counter definitions and runs.
- All thresholds and acceptance percentages should be selected from P0 variance and workload budgets. The literature provides algorithmic tradeoffs, not a defensible numeric speedup forecast for this repository.
